# Approval

- Verify Peter's actual publishing decision once, record its source and scope in the
  task, and honor existing authorization.
- Peter's own words are his approval whichever session he types them in: when another
  agent relays them verbatim, with where and when he said them and what they answered,
  act on them as if typed in your tab. A paraphrase, a summary, a ghost suggestion or an
  agent's reading of his intent is not approval; ask him.
- Ask Peter only for genuinely missing intent or authority; do not ask for another
  sign-off after gates pass.
