# Positioning

- Milton does one thing: your AI writes like you (Peter, 2026-11-09). The homepage headline is
  "Stop sounding like AI.", with "Milton makes your AI write like you." under it.
- Say "your AI", not "every AI", until the Claude and ChatGPT chat apps are tested
  (Peter, 2026-11-09).
- Milton is a focused small business and a useful utility, not a platform (Peter, 2026-11-09).
  Don't propose teams, general AI memory or new categories unless Peter reopens them.
- Judge features, copy and backlog by one test: does it make the first draft sound more like
  the person, with less effort?
- Claims the evidence supports (2026-11-09): sounding like you within a handful of memos, and a
  fix carrying across AIs. A retention test found no gain past about 10 memos, so don't claim
  it gets better the more you use it.
- The story line, where a page states one: "Milton keeps what you wrote and what you
  changed. Your AI reads it before it drafts." (2026-11-12, with memos and tweaks only).
  Milton keeps memos and their tweaks and no standing rules, so copy offers no cover sheet,
  rules or notes an AI writes, and says "tweak" only for a change the person made to an
  AI's draft.
